# Relay

## Register

product

## Users

A single owner managing personal projects, tasks, and commitments on desktop
and mobile, including returning after inactivity.

## Product Purpose

Make work easy to capture, organize, update, and resume: projects hold tasks,
tasks go on the calendar, one Today screen tells me what matters now. Three
screens (Today, Projects, Calendar) share one shell. SQLite records are the
source of truth.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, direct. Explain what happened and how to recover without inventing
progress, recommendations, or a working AI connection.

## Anti-references

No overwhelming dashboard, nested-card scaffolding, decorative choreography,
mock production content, or controls that only appear functional.

## Design Principles

- Correctness before decoration; preserve input and later work on failure.
- Consistent navigation and shared details across every workspace view.
- Make consequential actions explicit, previewed, and recoverable.
- Make every important workflow usable without dragging or AI.
- Follow DESIGN.md's paper-and-graphite direction.
- Importance is an explicit user flag, separate from deadlines.
- Confirm project completion cascades; pause, reopen, and archive preserve child statuses.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and touch alternatives, semantic labels, visible focus, accessible
contrast, non-color status indicators, reduced motion, and mobile usability
are required throughout. No specific certification is claimed.

## Product Authority

`docs/PRD.md` (Relay PRD v3) is the only product authority. Earlier PRDs and
phase documents in `docs/archive/` are historical reference and do not apply.
